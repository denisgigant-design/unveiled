Made whole.

A little study on body, soul and spirit.

“And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.”1 THESSALONIANS 5:23 · KJV

One person. Three aspects of life. This study uses body, soul and spirit as a simple Christian teaching model. Read each passage in context.

01 · BODY

Your physical life.

Our senses, movement and physical needs give expression to our inner life. Care for your body, and put faith into practical action.

Read: 1 Corinthians 6:19–20; Romans 12:1

Reflect: What is one practical way to honour God with my body today?

02 · SOUL

Your thoughts, feelings and choices.

Bring your emotions honestly before God. Let His Word renew your thinking and guide your choices.

Read: Psalm 42:11; Romans 12:2

Reflect: What thought needs renewing, or what feeling do I need to bring to God?

03 · SPIRIT

Your life toward God.

Jesus speaks of being born of the Spirit. Consider the new birth, worship and your relationship with God.

Read: John 3:6; John 4:23–24; Romans 8:16

Reflect: How can I make room for fellowship with God today?

The Bible sometimes uses “soul” and “spirit” in overlapping ways. These categories are learning aids, not a rigid definition for every verse. Teaching paragraphs are commentary, separate from Scripture.
